The shared encoder uses whole-pixel module scaling. This value needs 495
modules including configured margins. At 990px it can use 2px per module.
A 995px renderer probe produced 950px of bars with 22/23px white margins.
That probe is a bitmap measurement, not a deployed layout fix.
No physical checkout scanner was tested.
